agile vs non agile
agile pro: adaptability, transparency
Agile con: unpredictability, low flexibility
scrum master vs product owner 
Scrum Master: responsible for how team works. Focus on team health and efficiency
Product Owner: design for product vision and product management
3 Types of Meetings
Sprint Planning: The team decides on a goal for the next few weeks and decide on which to work on.
Daily Stand-up: a meeting to share what is going on.
Sprint Review: meeting where the team shows the finished work to get feedback and possibly talk about what they should do for next.
